Dear Students,

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to the Department of General Engineering. Congratulations on choosing engineering as your career path and becoming a part of the WIT family, which is globally recognized for excellence in engineering education. The first year of engineering is a significant milestone that marks the beginning of an exciting journey filled with learning, discovery, innovation, and personal growth. I firmly believe that every student possesses unique talent and untapped potential. Here, you will develop the confidence, discipline, and mindset required to succeed in your chosen field of engineering.

Our aim is to create an environment where you are encouraged to ask questions, think independently, embrace challenges, and continuously strive for excellence. Engineering education is not only about acquiring technical knowledge; it is about developing the ability to think critically, solve problems creatively, communicate effectively, collaborate efficiently, and uphold the highest standards of ethics and professionalism.

I am pleased to share that our First-Year Engineering curriculum has been designed in accordance with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which promotes a multidisciplinary and student-centric approach to education. The curriculum encourages experiential learning, innovation, research, entrepreneurship, flexibility in learning, and the integration of modern technologies with engineering education. These initiatives are aimed at preparing graduates who are technically proficient, intellectually curious, socially responsible, and capable of adapting to the ever-changing global landscape.

We encourage you to participate actively in technical events, workshops, seminars, innovation activities, internships, sports, cultural programmes and community engagement initiatives. These experiences will help you broaden your perspective, strengthen your personality, develop leadership qualities, and prepare you for future challenges.

The faculty members of the Department of General Engineering are committed to nurturing your academic excellence, professional competence, and holistic development throughout your first year. The transition from school to engineering education is a significant milestone, and I understand that it may bring both excitement and new challenges. Never hesitate to seek guidance from your teachers and mentors, who are always ready to support and inspire you throughout your academic journey. Remember, every challenge you overcome becomes a stepping stone toward your success.

Once again, I warmly welcome you to the Department of General Engineering and wish you a rewarding and memorable engineering journey. Make the most of every opportunity, remain disciplined, think innovatively, and believe in your abilities. Let your passion for learning, perseverance, and integrity guide you toward excellence.

With best wishes,

Dr. N.S. Gramopadhye

About Department

The Department of General Engineering serves as the academic foundation of engineering education by providing students with a strong base in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Communication Skills, Environmental Studies, and other interdisciplinary courses. As the gateway to all engineering disciplines, the department is committed to academic excellence, innovation, and experiential learning, enabling students to build a strong foundation in scientific knowledge, analytical thinking, technical aptitude, and professional skills essential for their engineering journey. Guided by the principles of Outcome-Based Education (OBE), innovative teaching-learning practices, and continuous assessment, the department creates an environment that fosters intellectual curiosity, academic excellence, and lifelong learning.

The department is supported by a team of highly qualified and experienced faculty members who continuously enhance their expertise through research, Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs), workshops, conferences, NPTEL certifications, and industry–academia interactions. The department is equipped with well-established Physics, Chemistry, and Language Laboratories, ICT-enabled classrooms, digital learning resources, and modern teaching aids that facilitate effective teaching and learning.

A well-planned Academic Calendar, a structured mentoring system, remedial classes, academic counselling, and continuous evaluation ensure effective academic delivery and continuous improvement in student performance. The department also conducts the Student Induction Programme, orientation sessions, guest lectures, technical competitions, communication skill development programmes, personality development sessions, value-added courses, and a Science Exhibition on the occasion of National Science Day. This exhibition provides first-year engineering students with a platform to showcase innovative scientific models and projects, thereby promoting scientific temper, creativity, innovation, and a spirit of inquiry.

Through its student-centric approach, continuous quality improvement initiatives, and commitment to innovation, ethical values, and holistic education, the Department of General Engineering prepares students to become technically competent, socially responsible, ethically grounded, and globally competitive engineers capable of excelling in higher education, research, entrepreneurship, industry, and service to society. The Department of General Engineering strives to lay a strong academic, professional, and ethical foundation that empowers every student to excel in the dynamic world of engineering and technology. With its unwavering commitment to quality education, innovation, and holistic development, the department continues to nurture competent engineers, responsible citizens, and lifelong learners who are well-equipped to meet the challenges of the future.

Dr. Gramopadhye N. S.
HOD, First Year
