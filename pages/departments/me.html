Our department has consistently maintained a tradition of academic excellence, outstanding university results, and successful placements in leading multinational companies. Over the years, we have built strong collaborations with industry leaders including Danfoss India Innovation Centre, Sandvik, Dassault Systèmes, Tata Technologies, Atlas Copco, IGUS, and Consistent Engineers (Dubai). This unique facility provides opportunities for product testing, research, consultancy, and innovation in fluid power engineering. Similarly, our Advanced Manufacturing Centre of Excellence with Sandvik and Mastercam enables students to gain expertise in digital manufacturing, CNC programming, CAD/CAM, and smart production technologies.

Innovation remains the cornerstone of our department. Our students actively participate in prestigious national competitions such as Smart India Hackathon, SAE BAJA, SUPRA, TIFAN, and various innovation challenges. They also undertake multidisciplinary projects in Digital Twin Technology, Mechatronics, Artificial Intelligence, Electric Mobility, Reverse Engineering, and Product Development, preparing them for the challenges of modern engineering. Faculty members have undertaken projects with La Foundation Dassault Systems.

Our department has been recognised nationally by AICTE–CII as the the "Best Industry Linked Mechanical Engineering Department" and continues to strengthen industry engagement through consultancy, sponsored projects, faculty development, technology transfer, and collaborative research.

Behind every achievement is a dedicated team of highly qualified faculty members, enthusiastic students, supportive alumni, and visionary management.

Together, we strive to nurture technically competent, ethically responsible, and socially conscious engineers who are ready to contribute to industry and society.

About Department

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at Walchand Institute of Technology (WIT), Solapur is committed to producing competent mechanical engineers through quality education, industry engagement, research, and innovation. Since its establishment, the department has built a strong reputation for academic excellence, skilled graduates, and successful placements in leading national and multinational industries.



The department offers a B.Tech. programme in Mechanical & Automation Engineering with an approved intake of 60 students, a M.Tech. programme in Design Engineering, and is also a recognized Research Centre offering Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering. The department provides an intellectually stimulating environment that encourages students and research scholars to pursue excellence in academics, innovation, and applied research.



A defining strength of the department is its strong industry–academia collaboration, which bridges the gap between engineering education and industrial practice. The department has established strategic partnerships with leading organizations including Danfoss India Innovation Centre, Sandvik, Dassault Systèmes, Tata Technologies, Atlas Copco, IGUS, and Consistent Engineers (Dubai). These collaborations provide students and faculty with opportunities for industry-certified training, internships, collaborative research, consultancy, technology transfer, faculty development, and placements.



The department has established the Intelligent Hydraulic Systems Centre of Excellence in collaboration with Danfoss India Innovation Centre, housing a 150 kW Fluid Power Test Stand and a Mechatronics Product Learning Centre. This state-of-the-art facility supports performance evaluation, endurance testing, product validation, consultancy, and applied research in fluid power engineering. It offers students a unique opportunity to gain practical experience using industrial-grade equipment that is rarely available in academic institutions.



To strengthen advanced manufacturing education, the department has also established the Advanced Manufacturing Centre of Excellence in collaboration with Sandvik and Mastercam.Through industry-integrated certification programmes and hands-on training, students acquire expertise in CAD/CAM, CNC programming, digital manufacturing, and modern production technologies. The department has also integrated industry-supported certification courses such as Intelligent Hydraulic Systems and Advanced Manufacturing Technology into its curriculum, enabling students to develop skills aligned with current industrial needs.



The department actively promotes teaching, research, and innovation in emerging areas such as Industry 4.0, Machine Learning, Digital Twin, Industrial Automation, Additive Manufacturing, Reverse Engineering, Digital Manufacturing, and Product Design. Faculty members and research scholars are actively engaged in sponsored research projects, consultancy assignments, publications, patents, and collaborative research with industry and academic institutions.



The department has received national recognition for its sustained efforts in strengthening industry engagement. It was conferred the prestigious AICTE–Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) Award as the "Best Industry Linked Mechanical Engineering Department" for the academic year 2014-15 2014–15 and was recognized as a Finalist during 2020-21, reaffirming its commitment to excellence in industry–academia collaboration.



Supported by highly qualified and dedicated faculty members, modern laboratories, research facilities, and strong industrial partnerships, the Department of Mechanical Engineering continues its journey towards becoming a Centre of Excellence in Mechanical Engineering. The department remains committed to nurturing technically competent, innovative, ethically responsible, and globally competitive engineers capable of addressing the technological challenges of the future
