About Us

Education is Religion — a legacy of technical excellence since 1983.

Walchand Institute of Technology (WIT), Solapur, established in 1983, is a premier unaided professional institution named after visionary industrialist Seth Walchand Hirachand. Approved by AICTE and DTE Maharashtra, and affiliated to Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Solapur University, the institute has been conferred autonomous status from 2021–22, empowering it to design its own curriculum and conduct examinations.

Accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade and with all UG programs accredited by NBA, WIT offers six undergraduate engineering programs, four postgraduate courses, and recognized Ph.D. research centers in Civil, Mechanical, Electronics, and Computer Science Engineering. Spread across 22 acres of lush, Wi-Fi-enabled campus, the institute boasts state-of-the-art laboratories, modern computational facilities, and excellent sports infrastructure including an athletic track, football, volleyball, and cricket grounds. Hostel facilities accommodate 260 girls and 750 boys, ensuring a vibrant residential life.

With highly qualified faculty, strong industry linkages, and a consistent record of academic excellence, WIT has achieved outstanding campus placements, with students securing top positions and competitive salary packages. The institute also provides consultancy services across disciplines and is committed to building a sustainable campus. WIT continues to nurture innovation, holistic development, and professional success, making it a preferred destination for aspiring engineers.
